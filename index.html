Welcome to Hekima Voice

Hekima Voice (HV) is a non-profit, non-governmental organization (NGO) dedicated to protecting the rights, dignity, and safety of adolescent girls and young women (AGYW). Our programs are designed to promote health, education, and green economic empowerment, supporting girls and women to develop sustainable livelihoods and access safer, more independent, and empowered futures.

Our Vision

To create a safe and empowered environment where adolescent girls and young women can live with dignity, exercise their rights, and build independent and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To amplify the voices of adolescent girls and young women, advocate for their rights, address labour and sexual exploitation, and promote health, education and green economic empowerment.

What We Do

Education

We provide girls and adolescents with access to education, skills training, and practical learning opportunities.

Economic Empowerment

We support out-of-school adolescent girls and young women (AGYW) to develop practical skills that can help them build sustainable livelihoods.

Child Protection & Equality

We work to protect adolescent girls from child marriage, child labour, trafficking, violence, and school dropout.

Green Innovation

We support adolescent girls and young women to understand environmental challenges, climate change, and sustainable practices.
