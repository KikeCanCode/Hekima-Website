Our Vision

To create a just, safe and inclusive society where every child, adolescent girl and young woman is protected from trafficking, exploitation, violence and abuse, and empowered to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To end the cycle of trafficking, exploitation and violence by protecting rights, amplifying voices and strengthening the leadership of children, adolescent girls and young women through advocacy, protection and empowerment.

Our Values

🛡️ Protection
💜 Empowerment
⚖️ Rights
📚 Education
🤝 Equality
