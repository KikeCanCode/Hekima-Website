Education

We provide girls and adolescents with access to education, skills training, and practical learning opportunities that support their personal and future development. Our programmes help girls stay in school, build confidence, develop practical skills, and gain the knowledge and support they need to pursue further education, employment, and sustainable livelihoods.

Economic Empowerment

We support out-of-school adolescent girls and young women (AGYW) to develop practical skills that can help them build sustainable livelihoods. Through entrepreneurship and business training, participants gain knowledge in business planning, marketing, customer care, and small-business management. They also learn from women-led businesses and receive guidance to start or strengthen their own income-generating activities.


Child Protection & Equality

We work to protect adolescent girls from child marriage, child labour, trafficking, violence, and school dropout. We promote equality by helping girls understand their rights, access education, and develop the skills and confidence they need to participate fully in their communities.

Green Innovation

We support adolescent girls and young women to understand environmental challenges, climate change, and sustainable practices. Through recycling, green entrepreneurship, and practical skills, participants explore ways to protect the environment while creating sustainable income opportunities.
