About Us

Hekima Voice (HV) is a non-profit, non-governmental organization (NGO) founded and registered in 2014 under the NGO Act No. 24 of 2002, with its headquarters in Bukoba, Kagera, Tanzania.

Hekima Voice focuses on empowering adolescent girls and young women (AGYW), particularly those affected by or at risk of child marriage, labour exploitation, domestic work, gender-based violence, trafficking, and limited access to education and livelihood opportunities.

Our programmes focus on preventing human trafficking, reducing vulnerability, and strengthening individual and community resilience through community-led and gender-responsive approaches.

By promoting cultural exchange, green entrepreneurship, innovative solutions, and appropriate technologies, we expand opportunities for vulnerable communities, foster self-reliance, and contribute to lasting positive change.

Our Vision

To create a safe and empowered environment where adolescent girls and young women can live with dignity, exercise their rights, and build independent and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To amplify the voices of adolescent girls and young women, advocate for their rights, address labour and sexual exploitation, and promote health, education and green economic empowerment.

Our Programmes

1. Education

We provide girls and adolescents with access to education, skills training, and practical learning opportunities that support their personal and future development. Our programmes help girls stay in school, build confidence, develop practical skills, and gain the knowledge and support they need to pursue further education, employment, and sustainable livelihoods.

2. Economic Empowerment

We support out-of-school adolescent girls and young women (AGYW) to develop practical skills that can help them build sustainable livelihoods. Through entrepreneurship and business training, participants gain knowledge in business planning, and small-business management. They also learn from women-led businesses and receive guidance to start or strengthen their own income-generating activities.

3. Child Protection & Equality

We about to protect adolescent girls from child marriage, child labour, trafficking, violence, and school dropout. We promote equality by helping girls understand their rights, access education, and develop the skills and confidence they need to participate fully in their communities.

4. Green Innovation

We support adolescent girls and young women to understand environmental challenges, climate change, and sustainable practices. Through recycling, green entrepreneurship, and practical skills, participants explore ways to protect the environment while creating sustainable income opportunities.
