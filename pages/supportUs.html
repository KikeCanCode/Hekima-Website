How you can support our work

Your support powers our mission. Volunteer or donate to help us educate and create safer, more empowered futures for young women.


Volunteer Your Skills

There are many ways to support Hekima Voice through your time, skills and experience. We welcome people who would like to contribute to our work and support our programmes. Whether you have experience in education, communications, digital skills, business, community development, fundraising, administration, or other areas, your knowledge and time can help us create positive opportunities for adolescent girls and young women. If you would like to volunteer or discuss how your skills could support our work, please get in touch with us.

Make a Donation

Your support can help Hekima Voice continue providing education, practical skills, advocacy and livelihood opportunities for adolescent girls and young women. Contributions can help us strengthen our programmes, reach vulnerable communities and create opportunities that support safer, more independent and empowered futures. Every contribution, big or small, can help us continue our work and make a difference in the communities we serve. If you would like to support Hekima Voice financially, please contact us to find out how you can make a contribution.
