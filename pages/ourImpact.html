Hekima Voice's Impacts

Women's Protection &
Economic Empowerment

Hekima Voice is committed to protecting the rights and dignity of adolescent girls and young women in Tanzania. Through protection, advocacy, education and economic empowerment, we support vulnerable communities to overcome challenges, build resilience and create safer, more independent futures.


Our Achievements

101

People supported towards
safety and recovery

Hekima Voice supported women and children after experiencing domestic violence and works to promote safety, dignity and recovery. Our programmes help vulnerable individuals move towards safer futures and access the support they need.

80

Women supported to establish
small businesses

We support women to establish small businesses and strengthen their economic independence. Through ongoing support, we encourage women to grow and sustain their businesses and build more secure livelihoods.

890

People ccessed to Education
& Practical Training

We provided access to education, practical skills and learning opportunities for adolescent girls and young women. Our work aims to strengthen confidence, expand opportunities and support more independent futures.
